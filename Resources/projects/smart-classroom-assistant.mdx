## Overview

A multimodal classroom assistant that turns a camera and microphone into an attendance, engagement, and Q&A analytics tool.

## Vision Side

- **Face recognition** for automatic attendance, powered by the `face_recognition` library with OpenCV tracking.
- **Emotion detection** using a CNN (fer-style) to classify mood, feeding a class-level engagement signal.

## Audio & NLP Side

- **Speech recognition** (Google API) transcribes student questions in real time.
- **TF-IDF** vectorization classifies each transcribed question into topics, letting instructors see which areas students struggle with.

## Integration

- Modules run per-classroom session; results are aggregated into attendance and topic-distribution reports.

## Key Takeaways

- Combining vision and NLP pipelines requires careful synchronization and tolerant failure handling (e.g., poor lighting vs. noisy audio).
- Simple TF-IDF classifiers hold up well for topic tagging when the label space is small.
